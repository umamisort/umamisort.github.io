Privacy Policy

Umami Sushi: Sort Puzzle · last updated 30 September 2026

This policy explains exactly what the game collects, why, and what it does not. It is written to match what the app actually does rather than to cover every possibility.

The short version

We collect anonymous gameplay events so we can see which levels are too hard and where people stop playing. We do not know who you are. There is no account, no sign-in, no name, no email address, no phone number, no location, no contacts and no advertising identifier. We do not sell anything about you and we do not share it for advertising.

What we collect

WhatWhy
Level events: which level started, whether it was completed or failed, how many moves, how long it took, and why it was lost To find the levels that are too hard, too easy, or where people give up
Booster and in-game currency events: which booster was used, bought or earned To see whether the boosters help or are ignored
Purchase events: which product, the price and the currency To know which purchases are useful. Payment itself is handled entirely by Apple and we never see your card, your name or your Apple ID
Advertising events: that an ad was shown or a reward was claimed To keep advertising at a level that is not annoying
Settings events: that sound, music, vibration or notifications were switched To know which defaults are wrong
A random identifier created on your device the first time the game runs So that two events from the same player can be counted as one player rather than two. It is a random number, it is not derived from your device or from you, it means nothing outside this game, and it is deleted when you delete the app

What we never collect

Your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, calendar, photos, microphone, camera, precise or approximate location, health data, or the advertising identifier (IDFA). The game has no account system, so there is nothing to sign in to and nothing to lose.

Where your game progress is kept

Your level, your coins, your stars and your boosters are stored on your device only, in a file inside the app. They are not uploaded and we cannot see them. If your device backs itself up to iCloud or Google, that backup includes them and is governed by Apple's or Google's own privacy policy, not ours. Deleting the app deletes them.

Who processes the events, and where

Analytics are processed by PostHog, acting as our data processor, on servers in the United States. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, that means these events leave your region. We rely on the standard contractual clauses PostHog offers for that transfer.

Purchases are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, which tells the game what you have bought so it can unlock it. Neither we nor RevenueCat receive your payment details.

How long we keep it

Event data is retained for up to 12 months and then deleted. Game progress on your device is kept until you delete the app.

Your choices

You can turn analytics off at any time in the game's Settings screen. Turning it off stops any further events being sent.

If you are in the EEA or the UK you have the right to ask what we hold about you, to ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and to object to it being processed. Because we hold no name or email address, the only way we can find your data is the random identifier described above, which the Settings screen displays. Send it to us and we will delete everything associated with it.

Children

The game is rated for ages 4 and up and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from a child under 13. If you believe a child has been measured, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
