Umami Sort: Sushi Puzzle

A sushi sorting puzzle for iPhone and Android.

Lift a run of matching sushi, drop it onto a strip that fits, and serve a strip once it holds one kind. Eighty levels, no timer, no pressure. The game works fully offline and collects no personal data.
